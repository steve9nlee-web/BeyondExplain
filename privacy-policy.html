Privacy Policy — 通勝 Tong Shu Daily Almanac

Effective date: 30 August 2026

The short version

This app collects no personal data. None. 本應用程式不收集任何個人資料。

What the app stores

If you choose a zodiac sign in the "My zodiac sign" setting, that single choice is saved in your device's local storage so the app can remember it. It never leaves your device, is never transmitted anywhere, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.
  • No collection of location, contacts, identifiers, or any personal information.
  • No data shared with any third party.

Network access

The app may fetch display fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). This request is served by Google and is subject to Google's privacy policy. All almanac calculations run entirely on your device; the app is fully functional offline.

Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and collects no data from anyone, including children.
