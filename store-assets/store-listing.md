## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Your daily 通勝 (Tong Shu / 老黃曆) — the traditional Chinese almanac,
beautifully redesigned as a tear-off calendar leaf, fully bilingual in
Chinese and English, and working completely offline.

For any date, see at a glance:

📅 LUNAR CALENDAR — the lunar date, zodiac year, and the year, month and
day pillars (干支) with the day's Na Yin element.

🔖 DAY OFFICER (建除十二神) — the day's presiding officer rendered as a
cinnabar seal, with a plain-English explanation of what kind of day it is.

✅ DAILY ACTIVITIES (宜/忌) — what the almanac favors and warns against
today: weddings, opening a business, moving house, ground-breaking,
travel and more, with every traditional term translated.

⚡ CLASH & SHA (沖煞) — the zodiac sign that clashes with the day and the
direction to avoid. Set your own zodiac sign and the app warns you
personally whenever a day clashes with it.

🕐 AUSPICIOUS HOURS (時辰) — all twelve double-hours marked lucky or
unlucky by the hour gods, with the current hour highlighted.

🧭 LUCKY DIRECTIONS — daily positions of the Gods of Joy, Fortune and
Wealth.

🌱 SOLAR TERMS (節氣) — the current term and a countdown to the next,
plus traditional festivals.

📜 PENG ZU'S TABOOS (彭祖百忌) — the day's two classical taboo couplets,
translated.

🔍 AUSPICIOUS DAY FINDER (擇日) — planning a wedding, a signing, a move?
Pick the activity and the app lists the next favorable days, automatically
skipping days that clash with your zodiac sign.

Browse any date — past or future — with a swipe of the arrows or the date
picker.

• 100% offline — no account, no ads, no tracking, no data collection.
• Bilingual 中文 + English throughout.
• Elegant dark cinnabar-and-gold design.

The Tong Shu is a living piece of Chinese cultural heritage. Enjoy its
guidance as tradition — for reflection and fun, not as fact.
